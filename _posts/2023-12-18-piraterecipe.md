Picture this. You're half way into a recipe. Your hands are covered in raw meet and sauce. You're behind schedule and stressed and can't remember what the next step in the recipe is or how much to pour. 

So you open the recipe on your phone and you have to scroll through a whole irrelevant story first while being bomabared with ads on a hella slow website. It's all a mess.

Pirate Recipe is here to remove most of those stresses. Bare minimum recipe. No fancy style. Just the recipe and the ingredients. Keep it simple!

This was made back when I was learning how to setup a database on a website. So anyone can create an account and add their recipes to it.

Yes, all the recipes on the Food tab on this site are on the food website. The original problem was that it was to difficult to write a short story for each recipe, so it had to be forgone.
